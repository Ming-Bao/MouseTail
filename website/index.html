Kia ora from my Mac

Push off the edge…

Just push off the edge

Arrange your computers like the Displays settings on your Mac. The cursor crosses wherever the screens meet, even if they don't quite line up.

Never gets stuck

Your Mac is always in charge. A lock screen, a frozen computer or a Wi-Fi drop-out can't trap the cursor: it comes straight home.

Clipboard and sound, too

Copy on one computer and paste on the other. The other computer's sound plays through whatever your Mac is using, AirPods included.

Private by design

Pair once with a four-digit code. Everything is encrypted and stays on your network: no account, no cloud.

Feels at home

A menu bar app on the Mac and a bar icon on Omarchy. ⌘C, ⌘V and ⌘Tab do what your fingers expect.

Nothing to set up

Computers find each other by themselves and pick the fastest connection. Wired beats Wi-Fi, automatically.

Get Kiore

Install it on both computers, then click Pair… on your Mac.

Mac

macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel

Download for Mac

Not notarised yet: the first time, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Linux

Wayland: Hyprland and Omarchy · x86-64 and ARM

curl -fsSL https://galengreen.github.io/kiore/install.sh | sh

No sudo needed. Or download a x86-64 / ARM package and run ./install.sh.
